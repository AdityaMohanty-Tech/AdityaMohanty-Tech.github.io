Hackathon journal
What I built, what I learned.


THERMIA: making urban heat visibleSmart India Hackathon 2026 · Team Yukti

The problem. Heat and air pollution change from street to street, but most city data is too coarse to show it.


Our idea. THERMIA is a hyperlocal digital twin that combines urban heat and air-quality data, so cities can see where people are most exposed and act early.


My part. I built our pitch deck and prepared for the live judge presentation, including answers to likely questions and a clear three-phase roadmap.


What I learned. A strong idea has to be explained simply, and it must clearly show how it differs from existing solutions.
